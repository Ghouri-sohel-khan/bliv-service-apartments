Abbasi Tower

Luxury Serviced Suites at Irram Manzil

The Residence

Located in the prestigious heart of Irram Manzil, Abbasi Tower offers customized short and long-stay serviced apartments that bridge the gap between home-like warmth and 5-star hotel perfection. The suites are designed specifically for corporate travelers, medical tourists, and families desiring absolute privacy and comfort.

Our expansive living rooms boast elegant modern wood paneling, premium furnishings, and floor-to-ceiling glass doors opening onto private balconies. Orthopedic support bedding, dimmable ambient lighting, and fully-equipped modular kitchens enable a seamless, comfortable residency.

Configuration Specs

  • Location Advantages: Proximity to major medical nodes like AIG Hospital, LV Prasad Eye Institute, Rainbow Children's Hospital, Apollo & STAR Hospitals.
  • Transit Convenience: Walkable Irram Manzil Metro station connecting directly to Hyderabad's commercial zones.
  • Kitchen Facilities: Equipped with refrigerators, microwave ovens, cookware, and RO water purification.
  • Support Systems: 24×7 water supply, active generator backup, gated security check, and passenger lift access.
